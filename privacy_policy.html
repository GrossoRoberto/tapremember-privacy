Privacy Policy — TapRemember

Last updated: March 12, 2026

TapRemember ("we", "our", or "the app") is a voice note application developed by Roberto Grosso (nuke.coba@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights as a user. By using TapRemember, you agree to this policy.

1. Data We Collect and How We Use It

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO permission)

TapRemember uses your device microphone exclusively to capture voice input for creating notes. Audio is processed in real time locally on your device via the Android Speech Recognition API. We do not record, store, or transmit audio data to any server.

Google Account Data (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect your Google account email address and display name. This data is used solely to identify your account and sync your notes across your devices via Firebase. Sign-in is entirely optional — the app works fully in guest mode with no account required.

Notes Data

  • Guest users: Notes are stored only on your device (browser localStorage). No note data is transmitted to any server.
  • Logged-in users: Notes are synced to Google Firebase Firestore, stored under a unique user ID. This enables cross-device sync.

Feedback (optional)

If you voluntarily submit feedback through the app, the text of your message is stored in Google Firebase Firestore. We use this solely to improve the app. No personal data is automatically attached to feedback unless you include it yourself.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs, or SMS data
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

3. Third Parties We Share Data With

We do not sell your data. We share limited data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Google LLC (Firebase Authentication & Firestore) — stores your account info and notes for logged-in users. Governed by the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC (Android Speech Recognition API) — processes voice input locally on device. No audio is sent to our servers.
  • Google LLC (Google Play Billing) — handles in-app purchases (Pro version). All payment data is managed exclusively by Google Play. We never receive or store payment details.

4. Data Retention

Notes stored in Firestore are retained until you delete them within the app or request account deletion. Guest data (localStorage) remains on your device until you clear the app's storage from Android settings. Feedback is retained indefinitely to help us improve the app but is not linked to identifiable user information.

5. Security

We use Google Firebase, which applies industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Access to Firestore is controlled via Firebase Authentication and security rules that allow users to access only their own data.

6. Children's Privacy

TapRemember is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Withdraw consent by signing out and clearing app data

To exercise these rights, contact us at nuke.coba@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
