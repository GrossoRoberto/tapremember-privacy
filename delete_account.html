Delete your TapRemember account

This page explains how to delete your account in TapRemember, the voice note app by Roberto Grosso, and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to nuke.coba@gmail.com with the subject "TapRemember - Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the Google account you use to sign in to TapRemember, so we can verify that the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm by email when the deletion is complete, within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your account record in Firebase Authentication (email address, display name, user ID)
  • All your notes and tags stored in Firestore
  • All feedback you sent, including any email address you entered in it

We keep no copy of this data after deletion.

What is not affected

  • Purchase records for donations or in-app purchases are held by Google Play, not by us. They are governed by Google's policies.
  • Guest data stored only on your device: remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

See also our Privacy Policy.
